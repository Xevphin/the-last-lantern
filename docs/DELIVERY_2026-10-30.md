# The Last Lantern — October 30 delivery

## Play

Open this project in Godot 4.7.2 and press **F5**. Main Menu → Start begins a fresh run.

- Move: WASD or on-screen joystick.
- Sword: Space or sword button. Face enemies before swinging.
- Lantern Burst: K or flame button. Costs 25 energy; cooldown is 2 seconds. Damages and stuns enemies in a 180-unit radius. Walls block both sword and Burst.
- Interact: E or the contextual tap button. Confirm shrine restoration with E or Confirm.
- Pause: Escape or the top-right pause button. Resume through the existing pause menu.
- Death: Retry Area restores health and keeps this session's discoveries and defeated regular enemies. Entering an area also restores health and energy. Start/Play Again resets the run. There is no disk save/continue feature.

## Complete route

1. Forgotten Village: collect the Echo and Memory fragments, then confirm restoration at the Sacred Flame Shrine in the east. Four Wanderers occupy existing lanes.
2. Optional: explore the three existing village houses for Guardian Sigil, Ember Vial and Keeper Charm. At the boss encounter these weaken DIVESH by 3 HP, refill energy, and heal respectively.
3. Leave through the northern village exit. In Whispering Woods, recover the relic in Hunter's Lodge and read the journal in Keeper's House. Three Wanderers and two Stalkers occupy the woods.
4. Restore the eastern forest shrine. Take the northern Beacon route.
5. Approach the Ancient Beacon altar to engage **DIVESH**. Sidestep the red charge warning, retreat from the shockwave circle, or use Burst to interrupt. Attack during recovery/stun. Energy pickups are on the arena floor.
6. After DIVESH falls, approach the objective south of the altar and press E/tap to restore the Beacon. The existing two-second restoration animation completes, then the victory screen opens. Play Again starts a clean run.

## Integration and preservation

The project's existing maps, houses, shrines, menu scenes and HUD scene were not rebuilt or replaced. Their scene files match the start-of-task snapshot. No background music was added. No commit, push or merge was performed.

A complete pre-change source/asset snapshot, including uncommitted files, is in `.delivery-backup/before-completion.zip` (excluded from Git and exports). No pre-existing source/asset file was deleted. The hidden dummy instance was removed from the playable Player scene; its original scene remains available.

Divesh's `shadow_guardian.gd`, `ShadowGuardian.tscn`, Stalker script and victory screen were selectively adapted from the supplied archive. Guardian charge/wave states are retained, with current-health checks, two-argument damage, the player's collision layer, melee hurtbox and progression signals connected to this project. Stalker speed/damage and Guardian wave warning were tuned to the existing 130-speed, five-heart player. No Divesh maps, project settings or autoload were copied over this project.

Character art is a small original SVG pixel-style set in `assets/characters`, rendered as Godot textures. Keeper, Wanderer, Stalker and crowned DIVESH have matching silhouettes/palettes. These are recognizable static sprites; elaborate animation was intentionally omitted.

## Android

An ARM64 Android debug preset is prepared in `export_presets.cfg`, with landscape orientation and test/backup/report exclusions. Export was actually attempted and **failed; no APK was built**.

Godot reported:

- Missing `4.7.2.stable/android_debug.apk` and `android_release.apk` export templates.
- Invalid configured Android SDK path: missing platform-tools/adb and build-tools/apksigner.
- No valid Java SDK path configured in Editor Settings.

Once those dependencies are installed/configured, create `builds` if needed and run:

```powershell
& C:\Godot\Godot.exe --headless --path C:\Godot\the-last-lantern --export-debug Android
```

Expected output: `builds/the-last-lantern-debug.apk`. Physical-device input, performance and APK installation remain unverified.
